# Gate G02: Research Approval

## Reviews
- output/02-research/secondary-research.md
- output/02-research/competitor-analysis.md
- Updates to output/01-project/assumptions.md

## Checklist
- [ ] Every finding has an evidence ID, source, page and confidence.
- [ ] Facts and interpretations are in separate columns.
- [ ] No outside knowledge or invented statistics.
- [ ] Public complaints are labeled as patterns, not prevalence.
- [ ] Competitor gaps are hypotheses, not claims.
- [ ] Evidence gaps and research limits are listed.
- [ ] Assumption updates cite evidence IDs and do not raise confidence without reason.
- [ ] Written in simple English.

## Decision (Project owner only)
- Approve: move to Agent 03 (Analysis)
- Revise: list what to fix, then Agent 02 runs again
- Reject: stop and add better research input

## Record
Update project-memory/project.state.md with the decision, date and notes.
