# HCM One: Provisional Personas

Sources: [pain points](pain-points.md), [approved research](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [approved brief](../01-project/project-brief.md) and [existing assumptions](../01-project/assumptions.md). These are role-based summaries, not observed individuals. Vendor workflows and public complaint patterns do not establish how all target users behave. Goals and needs are evidence-linked interpretations. No demographics or personal quotes were added.

## Persona 1: The Payroll Admin

**Provisional: based on desk research only. To be validated in Agent 08 (Testing).**

### 1. Name and one-line summary
**The Payroll Admin — Prepares and reviews salary changes, bonus inputs and settlement amounts.** This describes documented workflow demands. It is not a measured profile of every HR team. **Medium.** ([EVD-01–EVD-06](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-46–EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-57–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-01–PP-06, PP-14–PP-20)

### 2. Context
- **High for documented workflows:** This role works with payroll inputs, approvals and exit records. Vendor products document salary changes, variable earnings and settlement processing. Actual target-company tools and responsibilities are unknown. ([EVD-07](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-28](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-46–EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-57–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-02, EG-09, EG-20, EG-26)

### 3. Goals
- **Medium:** Calculate the right arrears for each affected month. ([EVD-01–EVD-02](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-01)
- **Medium:** Work from consistent attendance and employee records. ([EVD-03–EVD-04](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-02, PP-03)
- **Medium:** Establish who reviewed and approved a pay change. ([EVD-06](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-05, PP-16)
- **Medium:** Prepare bonus inputs without formula errors, duplicate employees or missing entries. ([EVD-49–EVD-51](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-14, PP-15)
- **Medium:** Reconcile the amounts owed at exit and the amounts to recover. Understand which company policies apply. ([EVD-57](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-61–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-17, PP-19, PP-20)

### 4. Key behaviors
- **High:** Documented arrears workflows calculate expected pay for each period and compare it with actual pay. ([EVD-01](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-08](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **High:** Documented manual workflows reconcile separately maintained attendance and payroll inputs. Spreadsheet workflows involve formulas and versions. These are alternatives, not a universal tool set. ([EVD-03](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-05](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Historical PF work depends on the company's contribution policy and the affected periods. ([EVD-25–EVD-26](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **High:** Documented payroll products include review and approval steps. Some pending salary and bonus approvals prevent further bonus actions. The evidence does not establish one approval chain for every employer. ([EVD-07](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-28](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **High for named products:** Imports prepared variable earnings or schedules them in payroll. Keka also documents paying, holding, voiding and partly paying bonus amounts. These are product alternatives. ([EVD-46–EVD-49](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **High for documented settlement workflows:** Brings together open salary, bonus, expenses, leave, loans and other exit items. Checks the company's leave encashment settings and notice terms. ([EVD-57](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-61–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **High for named products:** Completes required exit sections and payroll steps before finalizing settlement. Products document locking a settlement or completing it fully or partly, and downloading a statement. These steps are not a universal sequence. ([EVD-58–EVD-60](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 5. Pain points
- **High:** Past-month differences, inconsistent inputs, incorrect employee data and unreliable spreadsheet versions complicate the work. ([PP-01–PP-04](pain-points.md); [EVD-01–EVD-05](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Approval history can be unclear. Historical PF treatment can depend on changing rules and company policy. ([PP-05–PP-06](pain-points.md); [EVD-06](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-25–EVD-26](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Bonus inputs can pass through separate preparation and import steps. **High:** Spreadsheet errors and pending approvals create documented risks or blocks. Their frequency is unknown. ([PP-14–PP-16](pain-points.md); [EVD-49–EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Settlement combines several open items and company policy choices. **High:** Unfinished exit sections or payroll prerequisites can prevent finalization. This does not establish permission to hold separation wages. ([PP-17–PP-20](pain-points.md); [EVD-57–EVD-58](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-61–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 6. Needs from HCM One
- **Medium:** Reliable inputs and a result that accounts for each affected period. ([PP-01–PP-04](pain-points.md); [EVD-01–EVD-05](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Clear responsibility for review and approval. ([PP-05](pain-points.md), [PP-16](pain-points.md); [EVD-06](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** A calculation whose applicable PF policy and period can be understood. ([PP-06](pain-points.md); [EVD-25–EVD-26](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Consistent bonus inputs and an understandable basis for the amount prepared for payroll. These are inferred needs, not validated preferences. ([PP-14–PP-15](pain-points.md); [EVD-49–EVD-51](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** A settlement amount that accounts for open dues and recoveries. Clear calculation bases and clear reasons for unfinished work. These are needs, not interface proposals. ([PP-17–PP-20](pain-points.md); [EVD-57–EVD-58](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-61–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 7. Trust concerns about AI
- **Low — Assumption, existing AS-3:** Acceptance of AI preparation with human approval is unknown. Documented human oversight does not prove target HR acceptance. ([EVD-30–EVD-31](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-06)
- **Medium for payroll relevance:** The enterprise study and vendor data-boundary claims provide security context. They do not establish this persona's own fears. ([EVD-42](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-44](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Low for acceptance:** Vendor AI actions and AI labels do not establish autonomous bonus or settlement decisions, or HR trust in those decisions. ([EVD-54–EVD-55](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-06)

## Persona 2: The Pay-Change Employee

**Provisional: based on desk research only. To be validated in Agent 08 (Testing).**

### 1. Name and one-line summary
**The Pay-Change Employee — Tries to understand an unexpected take-home change by checking records and asking for explanations.** This summarizes qualitative complaint patterns. **Medium.** ([EVD-15–EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-07, PP-11, PP-13)

### 2. Context
- **High for project scope:** This is the Employee role in the brief. The role can view and question its own pay and tax records. It cannot change payroll or access other employees' records. (Brief: Section 2)
- **Medium for observed situations:** The approved complaints describe unexpected pay changes and attempts to explain them. They do not represent every employee. ([EVD-09–EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 3. Goals
- **Medium:** Understand why take-home pay changed and whether the amount is correct. ([EVD-15](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-07, PP-11)
- **Medium:** Understand PF, TDS and why net arrears differ from gross arrears. ([EVD-09](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-13](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-08–PP-10)
- **Medium:** Get an explanation when the original question remains unresolved. ([EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-13)

### 4. Key behaviors
- **Medium:** Notices an unexpected bank-credit difference. ([EVD-15](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Asks HR and may seek help in a public community. This does not establish a fixed sequence for every person. ([EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Compares available pay records when numbers disagree. Some cases also check bank or statutory records. These are current touchpoints, not proposed HCM One integrations. ([EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 5. Pain points
- **Medium:** The take-home change and its PF, TDS or arrears causes can be unclear. ([PP-07–PP-10](pain-points.md); [EVD-09–EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-13](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-15](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Conflicting records and incomplete explanations can leave questions unresolved. An older record can remain a concern after a correction. ([PP-11–PP-13](pain-points.md); [EVD-16–EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** One exiting employee could not understand a settlement recovery calculation. This case adds an explanation problem. It does not establish a separate behavior group or show how common the problem is. ([PP-21](pain-points.md); [EVD-68](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

### 6. Needs from HCM One
- **Medium:** A reason for the take-home change that the employee can understand. ([PP-07–PP-10](pain-points.md); [EVD-09–EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-13](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-15](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Medium:** Confidence about which payroll record and amount are being explained. The effect of source evidence on trust is not yet validated. ([PP-11–PP-12](pain-points.md); [EVD-17–EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md); existing AS-4)
- **Medium:** A clear response to an unresolved pay question. No response-time promise can be inferred. ([PP-13](pain-points.md); [EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-03)

### 7. Trust concerns about AI
- **Medium:** Reported pay disputes and mismatched records show uncertainty about payroll correctness. They do not establish an attitude toward AI. ([EVD-17–EVD-19](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-11, PP-12)
- **Low:** Comfort with AI reading salary and tax data is unknown. No personal privacy fear is invented for this persona. ([EVD-42](../02-research/secondary-research.md) is enterprise context only; EG-05)
- **Low — Assumption, existing AS-4:** Source-backed explanations may increase trust. Competitor source features do not validate that effect for employees. ([EVD-35](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-41](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-07)
