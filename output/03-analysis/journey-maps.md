# HCM One: Current-State Journey Maps

Sources: [provisional personas](personas.md), [pain points](pain-points.md) and the [approved research](../02-research/secondary-research.md). These maps describe documented experiences without HCM One. They combine patterns from vendor workflows and reported complaints. They are not observed end-to-end journeys from one employer or employee. Stage order is a synthesis, not a universal sequence.

Tools are documented alternatives. The maps do not imply that every user uses all of them. Confidence describes the evidence for actions and touchpoints. Undocumented thoughts and feelings are left unassigned. Highest-pain rankings are separate assumptions, not measured results.

## 1. HR Admin: processing a back-dated salary hike

Persona: [The Payroll Admin](personas.md). The documented problem is reconstructing historical payroll and obtaining review. The evidence does not establish the same process for every target employer. ([EVD-01–EVD-08](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-25](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-28–EVD-29](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

| Stage | What the user does | Tools and touchpoints | Thoughts and feelings (from evidence only) | Pain points (PP IDs) | Evidence IDs | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Identify affected periods | Works from a salary revision whose effective date affects earlier payroll periods. Identifies the periods that need recalculation. | Salary-revision information and previous payroll periods in documented arrears workflows. | Not found in the approved evidence. No feeling is assigned. | [PP-01](pain-points.md) | [EVD-01](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-08](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | High for the documented workflow |
| 2. Gather and check historical inputs — provisional main opportunity area | Reconciles attendance and payroll inputs. Checks the data needed to reconstruct the affected periods. | Attendance records, employee master data and payroll records. Inputs may be maintained separately. | Not found in the approved evidence. No feeling is assigned. | [PP-02](pain-points.md), [PP-03](pain-points.md) | [EVD-02–EVD-04](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | High for input problems. | 4. Review and obtain approval | Reviews the salary change and follows the employer's approval process. These are alternatives. | Not found in the approved evidence. No feeling is assigned. | [PP-05](pain-points.md) | [EVD-06–EVD-07](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-28–EVD-29](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-32](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | Medium overall. Named product controls are High; approval-history problems and general sequences are Medium. |

### Highest pain stage and why
**Assumption AS-10: Gather and check historical inputs is the highest-pain stage.** This is the provisional main opportunity area. Separately maintained attendance and incorrect master data are documented sources of reconciliation and pay errors. These problems can affect the later calculation. ([EVD-03](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-04](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-02, PP-03)

The evidence does not compare this stage's pain with calculation or approval. Existing AS-6 already questions whether reconstructing inputs takes more effort than arithmetic. AS-10 extends that question to the ranking of all mapped stages. It is Low confidence and Unvalidated in the [assumption register](../01-project/assumptions.md). It is not an approved strategy priority. (Research: EG-01; existing AS-6)

### Gaps
- **Low:** The initiating person's role, request channel and exact sequence are not established. No unsupported manager request or approval chain was inserted. ([EVD-01](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-32](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-09)
- **Low:** Actual tools, time, frequency and stage difficulty are unknown. No time estimate or emotion is assigned. ([EVD-03–EVD-06](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-01, EG-02; AS-10)
- **Low:** Post-approval handoff and communication are not mapped because the approved evidence does not establish who performs them or how. GAP-06 remains the main handoff record for Agent 05. EG-16 is only its cross-reference. ([EVD-08](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-28–EVD-32](../02-research/secondary-research.md); Brief: GAP-06; DEC-002)
- **Low:** Rule parameters and detailed calculation edge cases remain GAP-01, GAP-02, GAP-03 and GAP-05. This map does not resolve them. ([EVD-21–EVD-26](../02-research/secondary-research.md); Brief: Section 9)

## 2. Employee: noticing and trying to understand a salary change

Persona: [The Pay-Change Employee](personas.md). This map follows qualitative complaint patterns. It does not imply that every employee follows these stages in this order. ([EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-15–EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md))

| Stage | What the user does | Tools and touchpoints | Thoughts and feelings (from evidence only) | Pain points (PP IDs) | Evidence IDs | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Notice an unexpected amount | Notices that the bank credit differs from the expected take-home amount. | Bank-credit information. | The reviewed complaints question why the amount changed. No additional emotion is inferred. | [PP-07](pain-points.md), [PP-10](pain-points.md) | [EVD-13](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-15](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | Medium |
| 2. Ask HR for an explanation | Contacts HR about the difference. A TDS label may not settle the original question. | HR contact and the explanation received. The evidence does not establish one contact channel. | In the TDS case, the employee still asked whether the deduction was valid after HR explained it. This is a reported question, not an invented quote. | [PP-08](pain-points.md), [PP-09](pain-points.md), [PP-13](pain-points.md) | [EVD-09–EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | Medium |
| 3. Compare records and reconstruct the explanation — provisional main opportunity area | Compares available payslips, payroll records and bank credits when numbers disagree. Some reported cases also check statutory records. | Payslips, HRMS records, bank records and, in some cases, statutory records. These are current touchpoints, not new product integrations. | One reported employee worried that a correction and inconsistent payslip could affect background verification. This feeling is case-specific. | [PP-11](pain-points.md), [PP-12](pain-points.md) | [EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | Medium for reported behavior. Highest-pain ranking is Assumption AS-11. |
| 4. Seek further interpretation | Asks a public community for help when the explanation remains unclear. | Public forums and the records or payroll terms described in the question. | The reviewed questions remain unresolved at this point. No other feeling is assigned. | [PP-08](pain-points.md), [PP-09](pain-points.md), [PP-13](pain-points.md) | [EVD-09–EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16](../02-research/secondary-research.md) | Medium |

### Highest pain stage and why
**Assumption AS-11: Compare records and reconstruct the explanation is the highest-pain stage.** This is the provisional main opportunity area. The approved complaints describe several records being compared when amounts disagree. One case shows that a later correction did not remove concern about the earlier payslip. ([EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-11, PP-12)

The evidence does not rank this stage above noticing the change or asking HR. AS-11 is Low confidence and Unvalidated in the [assumption register](../01-project/assumptions.md). It does not establish how common mismatches are or how long they take to resolve. (Research: EG-03, EG-04)

### Gaps
- **Low:** The order of inspecting records and contacting HR varies across the reported cases. There is no observed universal journey. ([EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16–EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md))
- **Low:** Typical response time, query status transitions, correction steps and final resolution are not established. No successful resolution step is invented. GAP-07 remains the main workflow record for Agent 05. EG-17 is only its cross-reference. ([EVD-10](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-16–EVD-18](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-03; Brief: GAP-07; DEC-002)
- **Low:** Preferred detail and comparative stage pain are unmeasured. AS-11 needs validation in Agent 08. ([EVD-15–EVD-17](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-04)
- **Low:** Reported payroll distrust does not establish employee acceptance of AI or the effect of source evidence on trust. Existing AS-4 remains unvalidated. ([EVD-17–EVD-19](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-35](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-41](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-05, EG-07)

### Highest pain stage and why
**Assumption AS-12: Prepare and reconcile variable-pay inputs is the highest-pain stage.** This is the provisional main opportunity area. Bulk imports depend on prepared inputs. The evidence does not rank input work above approval delays or payment handling. AS-12 is Low confidence and Unvalidated in the [assumption register](../01-project/assumptions.md). It is a candidate for review and testing, not an approved strategy priority. (EG-01, EG-10, EG-20–EG-22)

### Gaps
- **Low:** The target company's original inputs, owners, handoff and exact stage order are unknown. The combined sequence and distinct input-checking step are assumptions. No sales system or team is invented. ([EVD-49–EVD-51](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-20)
- **Low:** Eligibility, proration, formula ownership and approval return reasons are unknown. No universal incentive formula or approval chain is added. ([EVD-51–EVD-53](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-21, EG-22)
- **Low:** Actual effort, error frequency and comparative pain remain unmeasured. Vendor risks do not validate AS-12. ([EVD-50–EVD-52](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-01, EG-10)
- **Low:** Current tax mapping and statutory bonus applicability remain unresolved. This map does not turn those rules into calculation steps. The approved research requires official verification before build. ([EVD-72–EVD-77](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-82–EVD-84](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-23, EG-24)

### Highest pain stage and why
**Assumption AS-13: Reconcile exit inputs and unresolved dues is the highest-pain stage.** This is the provisional main opportunity area. Settlement combines several financial items. Completion also depends on exit and payroll states in the documented workflows. ([EVD-57–EVD-58](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-61](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-65–EVD-66](../02-research/secondary-research.md); PP-17, PP-18)

The evidence does not show that this stage is harder than establishing policy or finalizing settlement. AS-13 is Low confidence and Unvalidated in the [assumption register](../01-project/assumptions.md). Vendor dependencies support the hypothesis, not its ranking or duration. (EG-01, EG-10, EG-26)

### Gaps

- **Low:** Employee access, statement detail and correction ownership after finalization are unknown. The single complaint does not establish an exiting-employee journey. ([EVD-59–EVD-60](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-68–EVD-69](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-27, EG-28)
- **Low:** Proposed GAP-09 remains with Agent 04 and Agent 05. Component classification, allowed holds and due dates are unresolved. The map does not treat product clearance requirements as legal permission to hold wages. Reported separation-wage and gratuity rules have an effective date of 2025-11-21; verify from official source before build. ([EVD-65](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-71](../02-research/secondary-research.md), [EVD-81](../02-research/secondary-research.md); EG-25; [DEC-004](../../project-memory/design-decisions.md))
