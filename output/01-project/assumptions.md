# HCM One: Assumption Register

This register uses [BRD Section 11.1](../../input/requirements/Payroll_brd.md) and [desk research](../../input/research/desk-research.pdf). Pages 7–17 of the research describe evidence gaps and ideas to test. These ideas are recorded for review. They do not add requirements. (BRD 11.1; Research: Product implications and validation gaps, pp. 15–17)

Confidence describes the evidence for each assumption. It does not confirm legal accuracy. Agent 01 assessed the risks and proposed ways to test the assumptions. These are not measured findings or approved decisions. Supported means that the cited desk evidence supports the assumption. It does not mean that target users have tested it. There are no interviews before design. Usability testing with real users is planned in Agent 08 (Testing). (Project config: Rules for All Agents, Constraints; Research: Evidence standard, p. 2)
