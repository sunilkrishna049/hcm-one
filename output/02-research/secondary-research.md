## 1. Summary
1. **High:** Past-month differences matter for arrears. Fragmented inputs and spreadsheet versions can create errors. The research does not measure how common they are. (EVD-01–EVD-05; R1: HR findings, pp. 2–5)
2. **Medium:** Bonus work can involve upstream data, payroll imports and approvals. **High:** Zoho and Keka document variable-pay scheduling, imports or bonus actions. Actual target-company processes remain unknown. (EVD-46–EVD-52; R2: Bonus findings, pp. 2–6)
3. **High:** Settlement workflows reconcile assets, leave, salary changes, expenses, recoveries and payroll. These dependencies support settlement complexity, not a measured processing time. (EVD-57–EVD-66; R2: Settlement findings, pp. 6–7)
4. **High for the PDF's rule claims:** From 21 November 2025, R2 reports a two-working-day deadline for separation wages and a separate 30-day gratuity deadline. It does not say every settlement component is due within two days. Effective date: 21 November 2025; verify from official source before build. (EVD-70–EVD-71, EVD-81; R2: Statutory rules, pp. 12–13)
5. **Medium:** Public complaints show unclear pay changes and an unexplained settlement recovery. **High for stated features:** Competitors document payroll controls and platform AI. These do not establish autonomous bonus or settlement decisions or user trust in AI. (EVD-15–EVD-18, EVD-54–EVD-55, EVD-68; R1: Employee findings, pp. 9–11; R2: Explainability and competitor evidence, pp. 2, 8–11)
