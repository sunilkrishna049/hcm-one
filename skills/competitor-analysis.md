# Skill: Competitor Analysis

## Description
Compares competitor AI payroll features from the research and identifies possible opportunities for HCM One.

## Role
Act as a product strategist who separates what competitors publicly show from what is only assumed.

## Inputs
- Required: input/research/desk-research.pdf (competitor section)
- Required: output/02-research/secondary-research.md

## Instructions
1. List each competitor named in the research.
2. For each, record what the research says it publicly offers.
3. Map each competitor against the HCM One flows: back-dated hike, bonus, settlement, salary explanation, TDS explanation, raise query.
4. Mark each cell as: Shown publicly, Partly shown, Not found in public evidence.
5. Identify patterns that are now common across competitors.
6. Identify possible opportunities for HCM One, written as hypotheses.

## Output
File: output/02-research/competitor-analysis.md
Sections:
1. Summary (3 to 5 lines, simple English)
2. Competitor table. Columns: Product | What it publicly offers | Evidence ID | Confidence
3. Flow coverage matrix (competitors vs HCM One flows)
4. Common patterns (what is now normal and not a differentiator)
5. Opportunity hypotheses for HCM One
6. Limits

## Rules
- "Not found in public evidence" never means "the feature does not exist". Say this clearly.
- Write opportunities as hypotheses ("HCM One may stand out by…"), never as claims ("HCM One is the only…").
- Use only the research PDF. Do not add outside knowledge.
- Link every row to an evidence ID from secondary-research.md.
- Use simple English.
