# Northwind Technologies: Fictional Mock Inputs

All names and data are fictional. This is a portfolio-demo fixture, not real company or employee data. The supplied company name is Northwind Technologies. Sensitive identifiers contain only masked placeholder characters. Employee IDs such as E01 and user IDs such as HR01 are fictional internal keys, not PAN, UAN or bank numbers.

Source: Project owner mock-data instruction, 2026-10-13; [BRD v1.2, Section 9](../../input/requirements/Payroll_brd_v1.2.md); existing [rules config](../rules/). No input, output or rules file is changed.

## Fixture context

- Tax year 2026-27 runs from 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31.
- April through September 2026 are processed payroll months. October 2026 is open.
- Monthly salary structures are the original supplied component inputs. DA is zero for everyone. No gross salary or other component total is stored.
- Every listed employee is permanent. E08, E10, E11 and E12 have the supplied notice and exit context. These are scenario inputs, not a live employment timeline.
- Attendance covers April through October while employed. E06 has no April–June attendance because the joining date is 2026-07-01. E03 has no July attendance record at all. October records are fictional open-month inputs, not processed payroll output.
- LOP is zero unless specified: E01 has 2 days in June; E05 has 3 days in September. Calendar days in each month are date metadata. Days present remain null because the instruction does not supply working-day attendance.
- The Engineering annual bonus request is exactly E01, E03, E04, E07, E08 and E09, for 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31. E09 has no performance record. The Sales request is E13, E14 and E15 in Hyderabad for 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30.
- No eligibility decision, tenure result, achievement percentage, prorated bonus, notice shortfall amount, settlement component amount or payment is generated.

## Unspecified input fields

- Payroll cut-off date, actual payroll processed dates, salary-structure effective dates, and actual days present were not supplied and remain null. No payroll calendar or salary-history validity is invented.
- Non-Sales employee locations were not specified and remain null. Sales employees have the supplied Hyderabad location.
- E08's completed notice days and waiver decision were not supplied and remain null. E10 and E11 use the given served-in-full notice with the existing 60-day company requirement; E12 uses the supplied 40 served days.
- Clearance completion dates, E05 query creation date and HR reply, and gratuity becoming-payable dates were not supplied and remain null.
- Null target or achievement for an annual rating record means those fields do not apply to that record type. Null rating for a Sales record means it is target-based. This is distinct from E09's entirely absent performance record.
- The existing query is a seed fixture with the requested In review status. The audit list remains empty as requested; no live sending or review history is fabricated.
